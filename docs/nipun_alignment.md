## 1. NIPUN Bharat Foundational Learning Goals (Lakshyas)

Under the Ministry of Education's NIPUN Bharat mission, foundational learning goals for Grades 1 to 3 require children to achieve basic literacy and numeracy by Grade 3. VaaniSathi directly maps every database record and lesson plan to these national competencies.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        NIPUN BHARAT FLN COMPETENCY DOMAINS                        |
+---------------------+-----------------------+------------------+------------------+
| Oral Language       | Phonological          | Print Awareness  | Foundational     |
```

## 3. Bilingual Classroom Lesson Plans

### Lesson 1: Numeracy Foundation (`LES_G1_NUM_01`)
- **Title**: गिनती सीखें 1 से 5 (ᱮᱞ ᱪᱮᱫᱚᱜ ᱑ ᱠᱷᱚᱱ ᱕)
- **Duration**: 20 Minutes
- **Pedagogical Strategy**: Concrete-Representational-Abstract (CRA) model connecting Hindi counting numerals with native Santhali counting terms (*Mit, Bar, Pon, Upun, Mone*).

### Lesson 2: Classroom Orientation (`LES_G1_OL_01`)
- **Title**: कक्षा के बुनियादी निर्देश (ᱠᱞᱟᱥ ᱨᱮᱱᱟᱜ ᱢᱩᱬᱩᱛ ᱱᱤᱨᱫᱮᱥ)
- **Duration**: 15 Minutes
- **Pedagogical Strategy**: Total Physical Response (TPR) method combining speech prompts, visual icons, and student physical actions (sitting, listening, standing).

### Lesson 3: Phoneme Awareness (`LES_G1_PHN_01`)
- **Title**: ध्वनि पहचान और अक्षर ज्ञान (ᱟᱲᱟᱝ ᱪᱤᱱᱦᱟᱹᱣ ᱟᱨ ᱪᱤᱠᱤ ᱜᱮᱭᱟᱱ)
- **Duration**: 25 Minutes
- **Pedagogical Strategy**: Multi-sensory phonics linking spoken phonemes in Hindi and Santhali with Ol Chiki script representations.
